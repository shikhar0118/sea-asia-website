OUR BEGINNING

A customs-first foundation, built in Madhya Pradesh.

Sea Asia Shipping Services LLP was established in July 2013 as a Custom House Agent. Customs formalities and shipment documentation formed the company’s starting point and remain an important part of its perspective on logistics.

As customer requirements broadened, the company connected that foundation with freight, transport, warehousing, and project logistics support. The work continues to start with understanding the shipment and coordinating the services that fit it.
