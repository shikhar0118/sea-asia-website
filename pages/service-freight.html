LOGISTICS BASICS

Common freight and logistics terms

Terminology can vary according to the cargo, route, and service provider.

Transportation & handling

  • Freight: goods being transported, or the charges associated with their transportation.
  • Container placement: positioning a container at a factory, warehouse, or loading location.
  • Cargo stuffing: loading goods into a shipping container.
  • Cargo de-stuffing: unloading goods from a shipping container.
  • First-mile transportation: movement from an initial pickup location into the transport network.

Delivery & port movement

  • Last-mile delivery: the final transport stage to the designated destination.
  • Gate-in: entry of cargo or a container into a port or terminal facility.
  • Gate-out: authorized exit of cargo or a container from a facility.
  • Transit time: estimated duration to transport cargo between locations.
  • Movement milestone: an operational event such as pickup, dispatch, terminal arrival, or delivery.

Please note: Schedules, availability, and delivery arrangements depend on the route, cargo, service providers, and operational conditions.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Freight and logistics FAQs

Answers to common cargo transportation questions.

What is freight and logistics coordination?

It involves coordinating transportation, container movement, vehicle scheduling, port entry, cargo handling, and delivery arrangements.

Do you coordinate cargo transportation from factories to ports?

Yes. We coordinate cargo and container movement from factories, warehouses, and other locations to relevant ports or terminals.

Can you help arrange container placement?

Yes. We coordinate with transporters and logistics partners about container availability, placement schedules, and cargo loading arrangements, subject to operational availability.

What is the difference between freight and logistics?

Freight usually refers to the goods being transported or the transport charges. Logistics is broader and includes transportation planning, handling, warehousing, coordination, and delivery.

Do you coordinate with transporters and shipping lines?

Yes. We coordinate with transporters, shipping lines, terminal operators, warehouses, and other logistics providers as required.

What is container gate-in?

Gate-in is the entry of a container into a designated port or terminal after applicable entry formalities.

Can you coordinate cargo pickup and delivery schedules?

Yes. We coordinate pickup, vehicle placement, transportation scheduling, and delivery arrangements with relevant partners.

What can affect cargo transportation schedules?

Road conditions, traffic, weather, vehicle availability, loading delays, port congestion, regulatory requirements, and other operational circumstances may affect schedules.

Do you support import and export logistics?

Yes. We coordinate logistics for both import and export cargo, including transportation, container placement, port coordination, and delivery arrangements.

Can you coordinate container movement between warehouses and ports?

Yes. We coordinate container transportation between warehouses, factories, ports, terminals, CFS facilities, and other designated locations.

How can logistics coordination help businesses?

It helps organize transportation, improve communication between service providers, coordinate delivery schedules, and address potential operational issues.
