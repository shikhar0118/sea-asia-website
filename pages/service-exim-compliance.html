EXIM DOCUMENTATION & COMPLIANCE

Simplifying international trade through accurate documentation.

International import and export operations involve documentation requirements, regulatory procedures, government formalities, and compliance obligations. Accurate preparation and review help support smooth processing and reduce the risk of discrepancies.

We coordinate with clients, customs professionals, shipping lines, banks, and relevant regulatory authorities. Requirements depend on the goods, transaction, route, and applicable rules.

COMMON QUESTIONS

EXIM documentation FAQs

Answers to common import-export documentation questions.

What is EXIM documentation?

EXIM means Export and Import. EXIM documentation includes the commercial, customs, transport, banking, and regulatory documents required for international trade.

Why is documentation important in import and export?

Accurate documentation supports shipment declarations, customs procedures, and regulatory compliance, and can reduce the risk of avoidable queries or processing delays.

What is the difference between a Shipping Bill and a Bill of Entry?

A Shipping Bill is a customs declaration used for exporting goods from India. A Bill of Entry is a customs declaration used for importing goods into India.

What is an IEC Code?

IEC means Importer Exporter Code. It is issued by DGFT and is generally required for import and export activities, subject to applicable exemptions.

What is an AD Code in export documentation?

AD Code means Authorized Dealer Code. It is associated with the exporter’s authorized bank and is used for applicable customs and export-related banking formalities.

What is ICEGATE?

ICEGATE is the Indian Customs Electronic Gateway, used for various customs-related electronic services, filing, document submission, and communication.

What is a Certificate of Origin?

It certifies the country where goods originate and may be required for customs, trade agreement, or preferential tariff purposes.

What are export incentive schemes?

These are government programmes supporting eligible exporters through specified benefits or duty-related mechanisms. RoDTEP and Advance Authorisation are examples, subject to eligibility and current conditions.

Do you assist with RoDTEP and other export incentive documents?

Yes. We coordinate supporting shipment documents and applicable procedural requirements for eligible schemes.

Can incorrect documentation delay customs clearance?

Incorrect, incomplete, or inconsistent documents can lead to customs queries, additional verification, or processing delays. Document review helps reduce these risks.

Do documentation requirements differ for each shipment?

Yes. Requirements can vary with the goods, HS classification, origin, destination, trade agreements, and applicable regulations.

Do you support both importers and exporters?

Yes. Sea Asia provides import and export documentation and compliance coordination support.
