PREPARE YOUR SHIPMENT

Documents for import & export clearance

This indicative list helps you prepare. Actual requirements depend on goods, origin, destination, classification, and applicable regulations.

Commercial & shipment documents

  • Commercial Invoice: goods, transaction value, buyer, and seller details.
  • Packing List: package details, quantities, weights, and dimensions.
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill: transport document for the shipment.
  • Shipping Instructions: shipment details required by the carrier.

Customs & supporting documents

  • Shipping Bill: customs declaration for export consignments.
  • Bill of Entry: customs declaration for imported goods.
  • IEC details: Importer Exporter Code information, where applicable.
  • Certificates & licences: relevant permits, certificates, and approvals.
  • Supporting documents: purchase orders, declarations, and other required paperwork.

Please note: This list is indicative, not universal. Final document requirements depend on the shipment and applicable customs procedures.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Import & export clearance FAQs

Answers to common questions about documentation and coordination.

What is import and export customs clearance?

It is the process of preparing and submitting required customs declarations and supporting documents, coordinating applicable formalities, and arranging cargo release or handover.

What documents may be required?

Common documents include a commercial invoice, packing list, transport document, IEC details where applicable, and a Bill of Entry for imports or Shipping Bill for exports. Other certificates or permits may apply based on the goods and route.

What is a Shipping Bill?

A Shipping Bill is a customs declaration used for export consignments.

What is a Bill of Entry?

A Bill of Entry is a customs declaration used for imported goods.

How do import and export clearance differ?

Import clearance supports goods entering a country, including Bill of Entry and cargo release coordination. Export clearance supports goods leaving a country, including Shipping Bill and Let Export Order coordination.

What is Let Export Order (LEO)?

LEO is the customs order permitting export cargo to proceed after required export formalities are completed.

What is e-Sanchit?

e-Sanchit is an electronic document submission platform used in Indian customs processes. Supporting document coordination is provided wherever applicable.

Do you assist with shipping line documentation?

Yes. We coordinate shipping instructions, Bill of Lading documentation, delivery orders, and related communication as required.

Can you help with licences and regulatory approvals?

We can coordinate applicable licence, certificate, permit, and approval requirements. Applicability depends on the goods, destination, and current regulations.

How can I help avoid clearance delays?

Share complete and consistent shipment details and documents early. Our team can review the paperwork and coordinate follow-ups; clearance timing depends on the shipment and relevant authorities.

Do you coordinate container movement and cargo release?

Yes. We coordinate cargo release, container movement, terminal entry requirements, cargo handover, and cut-off timelines as applicable.

Can you support both import and export shipments?

Yes. Sea Asia coordinates documentation and customs clearance support for both import and export shipments.
