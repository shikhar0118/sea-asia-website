SHIPPING BASICS

Important shipping terms

Common terms used when coordinating an international shipment.

Schedules & documentation

  • ETD: Estimated Time of Departure for a vessel.
  • ETA: Estimated Time of Arrival at the destination.
  • Shipping Instructions (SI): Details submitted to the carrier to prepare the Bill of Lading.
  • Bill of Lading (B/L): Carrier document acknowledging cargo and recording carriage terms; its legal function varies by type.

Container & vessel operations

  • Gate-In: Entry of a container into the designated port or terminal.
  • Cut-Off Time: Carrier or terminal deadline for completing specified shipment requirements.
  • Transshipment: Transfer of cargo from one vessel to another at an intermediate port.
  • Booking Confirmation: Carrier confirmation of a requested shipment, subject to its terms and conditions.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Shipping line & carrier coordination FAQs

Answers to common questions about schedules, bookings, and shipping documents.

What is Shipping Line & Carrier Coordination?

It is coordination of communication and operational activities with shipping lines and carriers to facilitate import and export cargo movement.

Do you assist with vessel schedule information?

Yes. We coordinate available vessel schedules, estimated departure dates, arrival information, and sailing updates with carriers.

Can you assist with container booking?

We assist with booking requests and coordinate container availability and confirmation. Actual availability and allocation are subject to carrier confirmation.

What are Shipping Instructions (SI)?

Shipping Instructions provide the shipping line with information needed to prepare the Bill of Lading, including shipper, consignee, cargo, container, and shipment details.

What is a Bill of Lading?

A Bill of Lading is a shipping document issued by a carrier or its authorized agent. It acknowledges cargo and records the contract of carriage; other legal functions depend on its type.

What is the difference between ETD and ETA?

ETD means Estimated Time of Departure. ETA means Estimated Time of Arrival.

What is vessel cut-off time?

It is the deadline specified by the carrier or terminal for completing particular shipment requirements before a vessel’s scheduled departure.

Can shipping documents be amended after submission?

Amendments may be requested depending on the document stage and carrier procedures. We can coordinate requests, subject to carrier approval and applicable charges.

What is transshipment?

Transshipment is the transfer of cargo from one vessel to another at an intermediate port before it reaches its final destination.

Do you coordinate both import and export shipments?

Yes. We provide carrier coordination for both import and export consignments, including booking requests, documentation, and vessel updates.

Can vessel schedules and arrival dates change?

Yes. Schedules and estimated arrival or departure dates can change due to weather, port congestion, operational conditions, or carrier-related circumstances. We coordinate available updates.
