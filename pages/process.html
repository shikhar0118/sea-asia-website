OUR PROCESSES

Good coordination
starts with clarity.

Learn how we begin a conversation about your shipment and coordinate the relevant logistics solutions.

STEP 01

Tell us what you need

Share your cargo type, origin and destination, timing, and the solutions you are seeking.

STEP 02

Review requirements

Our team reviews the shipment details and identifies relevant customs, freight, transport or storage considerations.

STEP 03

Coordinate the plan

We discuss the scope and coordinate the solutions applicable to your requirements.

STEP 04

Prepare documentation

Documentation needs are considered as part of shipment and customs coordination.

STEP 05

Stay connected

Keep in touch with our team for coordination as your shipment progresses.

The steps above are a general overview. Actual requirements and sequence can vary by shipment, route, service and applicable regulations. Our team will confirm the process for your specific enquiry.
