OUR CAPABILITIES

Solutions that move

Customs, documentation and cargo coordination brought together to keep import and export shipments moving.

01 / CUSTOMS

Customs House Agency (CHA) Services

We assist clients with customs clearance formalities for import and export consignments, ensuring proper documentation and coordination throughout the clearance process.

  • Coordination for import and export customs clearance.
  • Preparation and verification of relevant clearance documents.
  • Assistance with Shipping Bills and Bills of Entry.
  • Coordination with customs authorities and relevant stakeholders.
  • Follow-up on examination, assessment, and cargo release procedures.
  • Support in resolving documentation discrepancies and procedural queries.
02 / EXPORT

Export Clearance & Documentation

We provide export documentation and coordination support to facilitate smooth processing of export shipments and compliance with applicable customs procedures.

  • Coordination for preparation and filing of Shipping Bills.
  • Verification of commercial invoices, packing lists, and supporting documents.
  • Coordination of shipping instructions and shipping line documentation.
  • Follow-up on Let Export Order (LEO) and export clearance status.
  • Coordination for container movement, cargo handover, and shipment cut-off requirements.
  • Assistance with export documentation requirements, including e-Sanchit submissions, wherever applicable.
03 / IMPORT

Import Clearance & Documentation

We support importers in coordinating customs formalities and documentation required for the clearance and release of imported cargo.

  • Coordination for Bills of Entry and supporting documents.
  • Verification of commercial invoices, packing lists, and transport documents.
  • Coordination for applicable import licences, certificates, and regulatory approvals.
  • Follow-up on customs assessment, examination, and duty payment status.
  • Coordination for delivery orders and cargo release formalities.
  • Support in tracking clearance progress and identifying documentation gaps.
04 / CARRIER COORDINATION

Shipping Line & Carrier Coordination

We coordinate with shipping lines, carriers, and related service providers to support the timely movement of export and import cargo.

  • Coordination for vessel schedules and shipment planning.
  • Assistance with booking requests and container availability, subject to carrier confirmation.
  • Coordination for shipping instructions and Bill of Lading documentation.
  • Follow-up on container gate-in, vessel cut-off, and sailing status.
  • Coordination for amendments to shipping documents, where required.
  • Follow-up on shipment tracking, transshipment updates, and arrival information.
05 / COMPLIANCE

EXIM Documentation & Compliance Support

We assist clients in organizing and verifying essential import-export documentation to help reduce processing delays and documentation errors.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list verification.
  • Shipping Bill and Bill of Entry documentation coordination.
  • Bill of Lading and other transport document coordination.
  • Assistance with applicable certificates of origin and regulatory documents.
  • Coordination for AD Code and ICEGATE-related requirements, where applicable.
  • Support with documentation for applicable export incentive schemes and related procedures.
  • Assistance in coordinating documentation required by customs and other regulatory authorities.
06 / FREIGHT & LOGISTICS

Freight & Logistics Coordination

We provide logistics coordination support to help clients manage cargo movement between the factory, warehouse, port, and other designated locations.

  • Coordination with transporters for cargo and container movement.
  • Support with container placement and cargo movement planning.
  • Coordination for port entry, terminal handling, and gate-in requirements.
  • Follow-up on delivery schedules and cargo movement status.
  • Coordination with shipping lines, transporters, terminals, and other logistics partners.
  • Assistance with shipment milestone tracking and operational updates.
07 / PORT OPERATIONS

Port & Cargo Handling Coordination

We support clients in coordinating port-related activities and cargo handling formalities associated with import and export shipments.

  • Coordination for cargo and container entry into designated terminals.
  • Follow-up on terminal documentation and cargo handling requirements.
  • Coordination for cargo examination and inspection arrangements, where applicable.
  • Monitoring of shipment cut-off timelines and operational milestones.
  • Coordination for cargo release, delivery, and dispatch formalities.
  • Follow-up with relevant stakeholders to address operational delays.
08 / CLIENT SUPPORT

Client Support & Shipment Tracking

We maintain communication with clients throughout relevant stages of the shipment process to provide visibility and support operational planning.

  • Regular updates on customs clearance and shipment status.
  • Follow-up on pending documentation and approvals.
  • Coordination to resolve operational and documentation-related issues.
  • Communication regarding vessel schedules, cargo release, and delivery milestones.
  • Assistance with shipment-related queries and coordination requirements.
